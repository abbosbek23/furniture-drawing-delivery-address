Sales / Manufacturing Address and Drawing

Share a furniture installation address and its drawing from the Sales Order to the linked Manufacturing Order — no more separate Excel handoff between the sales desk and the workshop.

What it adds

  • Production Information group on the Sales Order: installation address plus one uploaded drawing, PDF, or technical file.
  • Sales Information group on the Manufacturing Order: the originating order, its address, and the same file to download.
  • Values are read live through Odoo's native sale_mrp sales-line link, so an edited order updates the workshop's view.
  • The workshop role downloads the drawing without needing any Sales role.
  • A Sales Order Line selector for manually created draft Manufacturing Orders.
  • The file is stored once, on the Sales Order — never duplicated onto the MO.

How it works

Sales fills in the address and uploads the drawing on the quotation. When the order is confirmed, standard MTO procurement creates the Manufacturing Order and sale_mrp records the sales line on it. The MO then reads the address, the filename, and the file itself through that line — read-only, and always current.

Installing this app also brings in Sales and Manufacturing through its sale_management and sale_mrp dependencies. It creates no new models and adds no new access rules; your existing Sales and Manufacturing permissions stay in force.
